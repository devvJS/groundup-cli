## Hero statement
groundup is the only CLI tool that takes any developer — from first idea to shipped project — without ever leaving the terminal, without ever making an assumption, and without ever leaving them behind.

## The problem (in plain language)
The moment before a project exists is the most expensive moment in the entire lifecycle. It's just that the cost is invisible.

- You picked what you knew, not what fit. Three months in you're fighting your framework instead of building features.
- You stared at an empty directory for an hour before writing a single line.
- Your CMS doesn't talk to your auth. You found out three weeks in.
- You made 15 architectural decisions in your head. Six months later you can't remember why.
- You started building before you finished deciding.
- You came back in a new session and spent 40 minutes re-orienting.

groundup fixes all of this before a single file is created.

## The flow (website version)
01 → You answer two questions. What does this do? Where does it live?
02 → An AI takes over. Asks everything it needs to know. Adapts to your experience level.
03 → A complete blueprint is generated. You read it. You approve it.
04 → Your repo is created. Your project is scaffolded. Your agent knows exactly what to build.
05 → happy building. ⚒️

## Who it's for

### The solo developer
Has an idea and an empty folder. Might be new, might be experienced. Either way — groundup covers them. The AI adapts. The blueprint documents every decision. The session saves when life happens.

### The small team
Every project starts with a shared, approved blueprint. No "why did we pick this?" six months later. New team members read BLUEPRINT.md and are immediately productive. The handoff problem is solved before the project starts.

### The enterprise team
Architectural decisions documented and approved before a line of code is written. Compliance requirements surfaced in the interview, not discovered after launch. Every project follows the same structured process regardless of which developer runs it.

## Key differentiators to highlight

### The approval gate
Nothing gets built until you've read and approved your blueprint. This is the handshake between you and your project. No other tool has this.

### The AI adapts to you
New to development? It explains everything. Experienced? It moves fast. Hit h at any prompt for plain-language help specific to your project. "Help me choose" is always a valid answer.

### Sessions survive real life
ctrl+p saves everything. groundup continue picks up exactly where you left off. No re-explaining. No starting over.

### GROUNDUP.md — the universal handoff
Every project ships with an agent-agnostic instruction file. Any AI coding assistant — Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini — reads it and knows exactly what to build and how.

### Stack agnostic, agent agnostic
Works with any AI provider. Generates context for any agent. No lock-in. Ever.
